



What: The creature will start off just haunting the player. The creature will have five different places it can haunt the player. From Left to Right there is: The Window, Bed, Closet, Door, and the Vent. At first we will control the
creatures actions through the timeline. Since one hour is only 6 minutes in real time, the timeline will span 6 minutes and the creature will have set actions.
However when we reach around the third or second level the creature will have a goal oriented AI that will allow it to make decisions.

Why: The reason why the creature will start off slowly haunting the player is to build tension and suspense but also allow the player to get used to the creature's pressence.

How: We will use the timeline for the first level of the game. After that the creature will have a goal oriented AI.


Notes of what makes good AI:

-Good AI Lets the player cheat
-Good AI Tells you what it's thinking
-The player should not know what the AI is thinking, but know its goal. To kill the player.
-However Good AI Is Predictable.
-Predictable actions but unpredictable consequences
-GOOD AI CAN INTERACT WITH THE SYSTEMS!! - This is going to be necesary because the creature will interact with the enviroment!
-The Creature does not want to be seen, it wants to preserve it's identity.
-Good AI reacts to player: for this game we want the creature to understand the actions the player is taking
-However this is a short game, so the AI should not be too complex and take too long to implement but the AI is the main fun factor of the game for me.
